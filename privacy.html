1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube to TikTok Uploader handles information when used to upload videos to the user's TikTok account.

2. Information We Collect

The app may process video files, video titles, captions, upload settings, and TikTok authorization tokens provided by the user. The app does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to process selected videos and upload them to the TikTok account authorized by the user. The app does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or third parties.

4. Token Storage

TikTok access tokens, if used, are stored locally by the user and are used only to authenticate upload requests. Users are responsible for keeping tokens secure and may revoke access through TikTok account or developer settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may interact with TikTok APIs and video download or processing tools as configured by the user. Use of TikTok services is subject to TikTok's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Any local files, logs, or tokens remain on the user's own device unless the user chooses to delete them. The app operator does not maintain an external user database for this tool.

7. User Control

Users may delete local files, logs, and tokens at any time. Users may also revoke app authorization through TikTok.
