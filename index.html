About This App

YouTube to TikTok Uploader is a personal automation tool that allows the account owner to download selected video files, process them locally, and upload them to the account owner's TikTok account.

The app is intended for use only by the account owner and only with videos the account owner owns, controls, or has permission to upload.

Data Use

This app may use TikTok authorization tokens only for the purpose of uploading videos authorized by the user. Tokens are stored locally by the user and are not sold or shared with third parties.
